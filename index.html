Short Info

I study mathematics and computer science. I'm interested in number theory, computational complexity theory, and algorithms. In particular, in Modular Forms and Group/Graph/Number theoretic algorithms (and their hardness). My motivation is explained here. I'm currently working on understanding the basics of enumerative geometry, motivic homotopy theory, and its quadratic enrichment towards counts in the Grothendieck–Witt ring.

I support human understanding in mathematics, and moreover, I support human-AI collaboration in mathematics. I explicitly oppose AHM, because no one is the owner of mathematics and some mathematicians did ask for [the] work to be done. More details about my position on AI is given in this page. I am currently not affiliated with any AI labs, and I currently receive no private sponsorship from any AI labs. This information will be updated whenever it changes.

I am currently looking for a PhD position or a job after graduation (expected March 2027). Feel free to contact me by email!

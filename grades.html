Grades

Here are my grades, in reverse chronology. Official academic transcripts can be provided upon request. I provide the grades here for transparency and in case anyone would like to know about my particular performances. Readers are welcome to form their own judgments about my academic performance. I do not claim to be an exceptionally talented student, nor do I believe that I need to establish exceptional talent to justify my desire to pursue mathematics. Regardless of how my abilities are judged, I love mathematics and intend to continue pursuing it. I believe that everyone deserves the opportunity to pursue mathematics, independently of their academic achievements or perceived talents. I'm not going to say anything more to avoid turning this page into a manifesto, but I recommend the reader who is interested about me to check out the main idea.

Letter grades mean the following:

Grade Interpretation
A+The course's academic objectives were surpassed and the student has gone above and beyond expectations
AThe course's academic objectives were fully attained and the student has demonstrated particular capabilities during the course
A-The course's academic objectives were fully attained
B+The course's academic objectives were mostly attained
B, B-The course's academic objectives were generally attained
CThe course's academic objectives were partially attained with adequate knowledge to move on to more advanced work
DThe course's academic objectives were minimally attained with persistent weaknesses
EThe course's academic objectives were not attained
FThe course's academic objectives were not attained as the student demonstrated no or little effort (e.g. numerous unexcused absences, failed to attend final exams) and/or committed/attempted to commit an academic offense (e.g. cheating, plagiarism).

Grades ranging from A+ to D are passing grades which allow students to receive ECTS credits. Grades of E and F allow students to take remedial exams. When a student passes a remedial exam, s/he can only receive a maximum grade of C. This grade replaces the failing grade initially obtained.
